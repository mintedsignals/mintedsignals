Polymarket is a prediction market where you trade YES and NO shares on real-world events through an on-chain order book, with balances and payouts in USDC, a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar. It lists markets on politics, economics, crypto, sports and culture.

## How Polymarket works

Each market is a question with outcome shares that pay $1 if they're right. Prices are set by traders in an order book: you can buy at the best price on offer or place a limit order at your own price. Trades and payouts settle on a blockchain. See [what is a prediction market?](/guides/what-is-a-prediction-market) for the basics.

Questions with several possible answers, such as who will win an election, are run as a group of yes-or-no markets, one per candidate.
