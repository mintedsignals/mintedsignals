Kalshi is a US exchange regulated by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) where you trade contracts on real-world events in US dollars. It lists markets on politics, the economy, financial markets, crypto prices, sports and culture.

## How Kalshi works

Each Kalshi market is a yes-or-no contract that pays $1 if the event happens. Prices are set by traders on the exchange, and you can buy at the best available price or place a limit order. Questions with several answers, such as the next Fed decision, are offered as a set of separate yes-or-no contracts. See [what is a prediction market?](/guides/what-is-a-prediction-market) for the basics.
