Polymarket and Kalshi both let you trade on real-world events, but they are built differently. Polymarket is an on-chain order book where you trade with USDC, a dollar-backed stablecoin. Kalshi is a CFTC-regulated exchange where you trade in US dollars. Which one you can use, and which suits you better, mostly comes down to where you live and how you want to fund your account.

## Market depth and choice

Both venues list politics, economics, crypto, sports and culture markets. On our [markets page](/markets) you can filter by venue to see what's trading on each right now and how much volume each market has. Deeper markets, with more volume and tighter spreads, let you trade larger amounts closer to the quoted price. See [liquidity, spreads and slippage](/guides/liquidity-spreads-and-slippage).

## How markets resolve

Each venue publishes rules for every market saying which source decides the result. The two use different processes when a result is unclear or disputed. See [how prediction markets resolve](/guides/how-prediction-markets-resolve).
